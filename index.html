About Me

I am Godfrey Inyama, a researcher and engineer working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, explainable models, robotics, and open technology. My work focuses on responsible AI, human–robot collaboration with Mixed-Initiative Dialogue, and open source AI research.

Currently serving as a Senior AI Engineer at Bengom Limited, I specialize in developing agentic AI systems, orchestrating LLMs for local inference, and building MCP Servers for human-AI interactions. My expertise spans Machine Learning, Data Engineering, API Development, Cloud Services (AWS, GCP, Azure), and AI model evaluation.
